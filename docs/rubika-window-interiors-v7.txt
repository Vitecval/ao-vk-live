Preview 20 Rubi-Ka exterior windows v7

RVK_WindowDepth: 0 Off(default),1 Subtle,2 Strong. Existing settings retained.
Materials > Window interiors. No playfield restriction. Native Scene/Visuals,
bindless rendering and the existing mesh-ids.bin are required.

This extends the live-approved global v6/ICC experiment. The registry now has
95 reviewed window materials (83 glazed/interior profiles plus12 shutter or
diffusing-window light profiles),304 stock/HD pane records and451 positive
material/model witnesses. Native render eligibility remains conservative.

Selected rooms receive more indirect warm/cool light, particularly at night.
The light belongs to the shared apartment, so adjacent small/large/corner panes
see one consistently lit room. Small panes still crop a full-size room. Room
dimensions, furniture, upright orientation, interior door and bright lamp
emitter are retained. Quiet apartments remain dim. The existing deterministic
room variation and day/night response supply variety without flickering or
run-dependent randomness. This does not add animated light switches.

The accepted ICC297408 and296246 profiles deliberately retain v6 appearance;
the stronger indirect-light change does not apply to them. Matched Off/Subtle/
Strong ICC fixtures are required to remain pixel-identical to approved v6.

Added low-tech, shack, factory, Athenian, living-quarter and window-covering
profiles use reviewed masks and positive model identities. Closed coverings
retain their original texture and receive only a small light leak through the
reviewed gaps; no room photograph replaces a shutter. A reviewed shop-front
and storage-window profile prefer office furnishings.

Clamp and mirrored addressing are supported on reviewed global profiles.
Clamped pixels outside the actual UV tile retain original rendering. Mirrored
UV charts mirror the mask while the physical room stays upright. Newland's
reviewed, model-guarded fallback also supports self-lit/emissive windows.
Border addressing, arbitrary UV transforms, alpha tests and unapproved blend
states remain excluded. The existing ICC lobby blend is retained.

The registry is broad global support for identifiable exterior openings, not
a proof that every window-like surface in the entire game can be replaced.
Real transparent views into playable space remain real views. Walls, signs,
screens, doors, vehicles and ambiguous/unknown models retain their rendering.
Native Omni-Trade captures were reviewed; plain metal panels are not turned
into new windows. Exact accepted masks and preserved candidates are listed in
assets/window-profiles/catalog.json. Do not infer windows from darkness alone.

Large/curved volumes, inferred storeys and separate native visuals remain
approximations. Separate visuals are not merged into one dwelling. Tiny/far/
grazing panes may show only a wall/door/furniture fragment or averaged light.
Interiors are not enterable/collidable, and bloom is optical light spill.

No new raster asset. Same2.67MiB eight-tile surface atlas; pane table14,592 GPU
bytes (14.25KiB). No new global rendering pass, per-apartment scene object,
moving person or outdoor light. Off skips classification/initial uploads and
native bounds queries; hot Off can retain allocations. Dense-facade distance
detail and the8192-resource native bounds cache are retained from v6.
