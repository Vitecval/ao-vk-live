# Preview 23 performance audit — revision 33

The latest supplied F: captures were Newland at 03:12:53 and a Shadowlands floating island at 03:46:06 on 11 November 2026. The client used a GeForce RTX 2070 SUPER, 100% render scale, Ultra-derived settings, 80 m grass distance and density 12. Seven other AO clients were observed running from C: during the investigation; they were left running. This can reduce the memory available to F: and means these are not single-client benchmark results.

## Findings and changes

- **Smoke was exceeding its budget.** Around the Newland capture, GPU smoke simulation cost about 13–23 ms per frame despite a 1.5 ms setting. Cost calibration stopped at 5x and overdue volumes could bypass the budget. Revision 33 removes that low calibration ceiling, bounds cubic grid size, and spreads due updates using measured-cost credit. Smaller grids remain stable instead of resizing on every timing fluctuation. A minimum grid step can still briefly exceed a very small budget; the credit system bounds its average. The separate sand simulation retains its own policy.
- **HD maps accumulated across areas.** The log grew from roughly 0.5 to 6.3 GiB of texture allocation, with local heap usage reaching 8,318 MiB against a driver budget that had fallen to 5,580 MiB. This is strong evidence of memory pressure, not a quantified measurement of paging stalls. Revision 33 retires only replacement sets unused for more than 180 frames when the driver budget tightens. Original game images remain available, active HD maps keep their resolution, shared owners retain valid references, and a later texture bind requests the HD set again. Destruction uses the existing GPU-submission retirement path. Uniform auxiliary controls compact losslessly to one texel only when every mip is identical; colour artwork is not downscaled by this optimization.
- **Grass is still a substantial outdoor cost.** The run reached 14.47 ms per frame outdoors, with another window at 19.93 ms. Several hundred thousand blades and grass shadows can be expensive at 80 m / density 12. An extra distant-geometry experiment showed no measurable benefit in its controlled comparison (about 3.98 vs 3.99 ms) and was excluded. No distance or density reduction is forced by this release. Further grass LOD/capture work remains a worthwhile optimization target.
- **Floating-island material 1010004:207100 was misclassified.** Its native olive grass has only about 16% texels satisfying the old saturated-green test; averaging into the thumbnail rejects even more. The reviewed meadow material now accepts olive vegetation while other materials keep the stricter path/sand rejection. The fixture produced 4,739 blades at the exact platform height; stacked islands remain distinct and grey platforms remain bare. Retail visual approval is still needed.
- **Water and shadows also have meaningful costs.** Several water windows reached roughly 4–9 ms, and dense outdoor sun shadows reached about 4.8 ms. Scene submission can also grow with extended scenery (about 17.9 ms in the last busy scene). These remain settings-dependent costs; no complete water, shadow or native scene-submission rewrite is claimed here.
- **Ambient life and ship beacons are inexpensive in this run.** Their logged costs were about 0.00–0.03 ms. Regional tint and drift variation add no new simulation grid, dynamic lights or shadow casters.

## Verification and limits

The controlled multi-source smoke fixture averaged **1.09 ms** for smoke simulation with the 1.5 ms budget, compared with **3.27 ms** in its explicitly unlimited mode. This is a focused budget comparison, not an old/new retail FPS result. The cache fixture verifies cold shared-set eviction, protection of an actively drawn set, successful reload and exact constant-map compaction. Threaded publication still rejects invalid GPU wrappers and keeps valid shared maps. Its fixture now creates only its own Vulkan device, avoiding two full graphics contexts in the 32-bit test process.

Terrain, olive/green/stacked island grass, manufactured-ground rejection and regional ambient fixtures passed. Build and typed grass/budget checks passed. Exact package preflight and installer recovery/download/launcher checks are recorded separately with the final package hashes.

Frame captures include readbacks and image writing; the 600-frame average containing a long capture or zone transition is not a clean steady-state FPS sample. No new retail before/after FPS, prolonged play, all-zone certification or additional physical-GPU result is claimed. FSR/frame-generation synchronization and freeze safeguards were not changed.
