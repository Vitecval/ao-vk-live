Preview 20 Newland Window Experiment — lived-in homes and offices (revision 3)

RVK_WindowDepth: 0 Off (default), 1 Subtle, 2 Strong. Existing settings are
preserved by the renderer-only installer. Full HD ao-cyber-world artwork is
required. Original/Lite artwork is excluded. Newland playfields 566/567 only.

Ten deterministic presets: bedroom, studio, home office, sleeping nook, sparse
home, hero apartment, desk office, storage/work room, hero office, mixed studio.
Expected assignment is approximately 65% homes, 25% offices and 10% mixed.
Strong lighting is approximately 45% dark, 27% dim, 20% clear, 8% hero.
Subtle is approximately 50% dark, 30% dim, 16% clear, 4% hero. Finite samples
need not match these probabilities. Lit rooms are usually furnished; only 20%
of dark rooms contain furniture. There are no people or animated room changes.

Eight reusable textured room cards now carry beds, fabric bedding, desks,
chairs, cabinets, books, plants and lamps with baked contact/bounced lighting.
Their approximate depth maps support bounded relief projection: Subtle at most
10 march + 3 refinement depth samples, Strong 24 + 4, plus one colour read.
The ten presets reuse eight artwork tiles; storage and hero office reuse the
office tile with tint/brightness/mirroring variation. A procedural 3/8-box room
is retained as a safe fallback if the embedded atlas cannot load.
Room depth is capped at 0.80 / 1.65 world units. Day interiors are muted;
night interiors are warmer for homes and neutral/cooler and dimmer for offices.
Existing PBR specular and the restrained classic glass sheen remain.

Small outward optical spill uses the existing HDR bloom attachment and pass.
It requires HDR/bloom enabled; no wall material is relit or modified, and no
dynamic light is created. Glow is restricted to eligible window draws. Mixed
window/non-window groups split at glow-state transitions; this may increase
indirect submissions at those boundaries. There is no new global render pass.

Stable assignment derives from window pane, UV tile, and world-space object
bounds, falling back to transformed draw bounds. No independent animation or
wall clock changes the furniture. As with v2, identical coincident draws can
share an assignment and differently partitioned geometry can produce variation.

Coverage: 6052 (4 panes), 6055 (2), 6081 (arched window incl. rounded cap),
6059 (4 horizontal slots), 6060 (broad window), 6069 (2 vertical slots),
6086 (wide transom). Total 7 materials / 15 reviewed aperture regions.
The full family audit and per-model dispositions are in
newland-window-coverage-v3.json. Runtime masks use conservative glass insets.

6059 also appears on doors: require positive CURRENT geometry identification
as model 1416, 1418, 1420 or 99415. 6069 also appears on gate4 (14212):
require model 1419. Accepted identity types are 1010001/1010026. All geometry
hash candidates must be allowed. Missing, ambiguous or untrusted identity fails
closed. The debug visual-pointer cache is not used for this decision.

6092 functional display, 6096 text/sign, 6053 SUPPLIES sign, 6095 pipe panel,
6134 opaque closed blinds, 99647 small barred/vent-like inset, 6082 bar/vent,
6085 and 99649 diffusers remain excluded. Other cladding/trim/doors and borrowed
non-window materials remain excluded; see the exhaustive audit for each ID.
Full-HD art and catalog ownership are evidence, not proof of every live placement.

One embedded 1024x512 RGB/depth texture, eight 256x256 reusable rooms. GPU cost
with mip chain: 2,796,204 bytes (2.67 MiB) per device, uploaded once when enabled
in Newland. Off does not load it; hot Off retains an already uploaded atlas
until device teardown. No external asset installation or per-window allocation.
Source artwork, approximate depth, packing script, hashes and full generation
prompts are in assets/newland-interiors and tools/pack_newland_rooms.py. Generated with
the built-in image_gen tool; the user reference supplied style direction.
Draw-record size is unchanged; layout and atlas slot share the existing field.

Depth is an approximate single-view relief, not a reconstructed 3D room.
Hidden surfaces cannot be recovered; oblique views can stretch furniture edges,
and large camera changes lose realism. Lighting is baked into the cards, with
runtime day/night scaling and muted glass/reflection over it. No live scene
shadows or physically accurate spill are created. The original procedural draft
is preserved in v3-procedural-draft for comparison with the textured refinement.

Validation images are actual Vulkan output on controlled facade geometry,
not captured Newland gameplay. Synthetic geometry identity witnesses test the
shared-material guard; they are not retail model captures. Performance reports
are controlled GPU timestamps, not whole-city or multi-client FPS guarantees.
