# Local r48: rigid angular rock clusters

Natural object rocks now receive fixed, closed angular meshes around their existing surfaces. The catalog covers 143 rock identities, including 1010004:5955, 9863, 205836, 214375, 205675, 205838 and 202640. Ground boulders, cliff walls, cave walls and floating rocks use the same surface attachment. Floating undersides do not depend on ground height. Native Ground Rocks also use angular cut models, with simpler facets for small scree.

Geometry is generated once per source shape/scale/orientation/variant and retained on the GPU. Eight stable variants per source break up repeated instances. The cache shares geometry across rock material identities and is bounded to 32 MiB / 512 entries; each dressed source is limited to 24 cliff pieces or 12 boulder pieces and 20,000 added vertices. If allocation or capacity is unavailable, the original mesh remains. Clusters do not morph, shrink with distance, tessellate, or use parallax. Projection and mip filtering keep the photographed surface fixed at distance.

Cliff dressing skips upward-facing triangles and keeps added geometry below the original top plane where the source has vertical extent. AO's original mesh and collision remain. This protects main grass caps but cannot guarantee that every lower ledge, narrow passage or landing location is unaffected visually; a retail playtest is still needed. Decorative protrusions do not add AO collision.

`RVK_RockShape` keeps its stored value and now appears as **Angular rock clusters** under Enhanced Rocks. Zero removes added geometry. Enhanced Rocks zero or Original mode restores the original material and shape behavior. Normal-map strength still controls the new surface relief.

The Full pack supplies three external photographic maps in `randy-vk/packs/EnhancedRocks/`: 2K albedo, 2K normal and 1K occlusion/roughness. They are decoded on a worker and shared by all object rocks (48 MiB of GPU mip data). [Rock Face 03](https://polyhaven.com/a/rock_face_03) is CC0; source URLs, authors and SHA-256 pins are in `rock-face-03-source.json`. No Enshrouded assets are used. Lite gets no image files, no surface-pack downloads, and keeps original rock materials with optional rigid shapes. Native ground stones retain the terrain's shared cliff material.

This is a local F: trial, separate from the published Preview 24 release. `tools/install-preview24-r48.ps1` requires pinned successful checks, backs up the renderer, owned surface files and installation receipt, stages their replacement in a single recoverable transaction, preserves settings and the prepared texture cache, and refuses running clients or modified/unowned files. `tools/Revert-Preview24-r48.cmd` restores the previous renderer/receipt/owned surfaces and removes only the four newly owned pack files when appropriate. Published Preview 24 files are unchanged.

Validation and candidate files: `build/preview24/rocks-r48/production/`. Actual renderer fixtures: `build/preview24/rocks-r48/runtime/final-checks/`. The fixture is a representative cliff / boulder / floating-rock scene, not a capture of an AO playfield. Geometry checks include exact closed edges, deterministic generation, floating attachment, protected top planes, indexed list/strip/fan input, baked lighting, invalid inputs and bounded geometry. Runtime checks cover the catalog, direct versus registered geometry, far/moving cameras, shadows, dense scenes, unknown texture and character exclusions, transparency and missing Full assets. Installer checks cover Full/Lite changes, no Lite downloads, ownership refusal, repeated installation and exact revert.
