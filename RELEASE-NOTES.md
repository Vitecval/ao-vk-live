Rubi-Ka Horizon 0.7.8

A brighter, more detailed Rubi-Ka and Shadowlands.

• Expanded HD artwork for buildings, scenery, armor and creatures. All finished, reviewed textures are included with Full graphics.
• Shared Terrain+ keeps the familiar look from 0.7.7, with smoother hills, cliffs and ground transitions. Snow has more depth, clearer footprints and better-looking distant hills.
• Penumbra feels brighter and snowier while keeping its shadows. Inferno has softer lighting and a gentle sense of heat, with less red glare and a calmer lava glow.
• Grid destination signs are easier to read. Billboards and screens are steadier, and underwater lighting looks more natural.
• Optional Reworked Sound adds birds, gentler wind, regional ambience and underwater bubbles. It starts off, and your saved choice is kept.
• New Display controls offer supported resolutions, a monitor choice, and windowed or borderless play. Restart the game after changing these settings. Higher resolutions keep the game's usual interface sizing.
• Rocks and Scree starts at 0.5 for new settings. Your existing preferences are preserved.

Choose Full graphics to download the complete texture collection. Select AnarchyHD for the new world artwork, and Reworked under character artwork for the new armor and creatures. Existing verified texture downloads can be reused, and interrupted downloads can resume. Lite remains available without the large texture downloads.

Close the game before applying the update. Your saved settings and personal files are preserved.
