# Resolved HD terrain features

The paired hilltop screenshots from23November show a real loss of resolved structure with HD Ground enabled. The foreground grass region has6.858 RMS in the1.5–6pixel Gaussian band with HD off versus3.544 with it on. The middle rock region measures5.560 versus4.151. Those bands suppress one-pixel speckle; this is broader than a difference in tiny grain. The pictures have slightly different crop widths, so these are regional structure measurements, not an exact registered pixel comparison. All HD masters are detailed source paintings, loaded at their actual resolution. The master sampler already has trilinear mip filtering and up to16×anisotropy. There is no missing-upload or forced-small-mip explanation in these captures. Instead, HD mode deliberately replaces independent tile fine colour with a continuous material field, retaining only a32texel-per-cell mask. Its prior common4m photograph width reduced the fine grass/gravel structures to rapidly filtered subpixel grain. Averaging independent projection phases and material phases also reduced photographic variance.

HD grass/dirt now use an8m photograph width, and gravel16m; rock and sand retain4m. This resolves actual blades, clods and stones at larger physical scales instead of inserting procedural noise or recovering old low-resolution texels. Existing world-space projections, native transition masks and proper mip footprints remain. Phase blends preserve source contrast about the same mean with a bounded variance normalization. No sharpen filter, negative mip bias or native fine-grain layer is added. Alpha, original Shared+ geometry, path/seam guards, snow lighting and the independently owned underwater policy remain separate.
