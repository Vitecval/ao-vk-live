Preview20 Newland Windows v5 — upright shared apartments

Keep the v4 furniture, textured surfaces, bright lamps, interior door/frame/handle
and native true-vertex bounds correction. Do not fit a room picture to a pane.

The user's live v4 review found wrongly rotated/flipped contents, disconnected
light states across one bay and some blank-looking windows. V5 creates an upright
metric room frame using world gravity and the house's horizontal orientation.
All eight true-model bounds corners are projected into this frame. Native
negative-Y, Z-up, rotated and scaled model coordinates cannot turn the furniture
upside down. This does not rotate or modify the real building or its textures.

One apartment spans the whole native visual's horizontal footprint on a storey.
The arbitrary X/Z 4.8-unit room cuts are removed. Three front windows and their
corner pane therefore view the same furniture, door, lamp, wall and floor from
different positions. Window width never scales the bed, room or other objects.
Small windows clip that same space and show less. The floor grid targets 5-unit
storeys; a sill/ceiling safeguard keeps an estimated ceiling out of an opening.
The floor plan is inferred, not an exact reconstruction of hidden retail rooms.
Separate native building visuals still have independent apartments.

The latest instruction supersedes v3/v4's unoccupied-room distribution: every
supported window now belongs to an inhabited apartment. All receive furnished
content, with variation from quiet/dim to clearly lit and occasional hero rooms.
Every pane of an apartment shares that state. Some apertures can reveal mostly
a wall, door edge or fragment of furniture; they no longer randomly disable the
interior. The user-requested bright lamp pool remains. Subtle keeps 16 furnishing
primitives, Strong 24; both include the same 5 door/frame/handle pieces. Strong
has four deterministic primary shadow rays, Subtle one. No moving people.

RVK_WindowDepth: 0 Off (default), 1 Subtle, 2 Strong. Preserve stored settings.
All seven reviewed Full HD family-5 window IDs and the previous playfield,
material, geometry-identity and opaque-draw exclusions remain. All windows means
these verified apertures, not functional screens, signs, vents, barred/closed
insets, existing doors, other cities, Original/Lite or unsupported draws.

No new raster assets: the same embedded 1024x512 material atlas, eight reusable
256px tiles, 2,796,204 GPU bytes including mips. Native true-vertex cache and
32-byte draw metadata remain as v4. No global render pass or dynamic scene light.
Existing HDR bloom provides optical spill, not a cast outdoor light.

Checks include the actual Vulkan 3-front-plus-1-corner bay with unchanged
physical geometry and texture under negative model Y and a Z-up model basis.
Off, Subtle and Strong must match their upright representation to within 32
pixels differing by at most one 8-bit level. Every reviewed aperture must change
in Strong; raw masks remain strictly confined to glass. These are controlled
fixtures. Live Newland capture validation is separately recorded.

Limits: whole-visual bounds approximate irregular/L-shaped house envelopes.
Very large models can have overly open/sparse floors; furniture stays full size.
Floor estimates can still differ for unusual window elevations or mesh pieces.
Tiny/far/grazing panes lose detail. User captures remain the final appearance
check; do not treat a diagnostic activation flag as visual success.
